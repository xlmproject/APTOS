About the network

Meet Aptos

Aptos is a Layer 1 blockchain focused on making Web3 more accessible. Its technology stack is built to support developers creating decentralized applications, digital assets, and new on-chain experiences.

The network uses the Move programming language and a design focused on performance, reliability, and safety.
